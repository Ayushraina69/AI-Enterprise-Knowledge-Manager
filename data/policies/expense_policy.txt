Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees can claim reimbursement for approved business expenses.
Expense claims should be submitted within 15 days of the expense.
Receipts should be attached to every expense claim whenever available.
Travel expenses must comply with the company's approved travel limits.
Expenses above the employee's approval limit require manager approval.
Finance reviews submitted claims before reimbursement.
Approved reimbursements are normally processed in the next payment cycle.
