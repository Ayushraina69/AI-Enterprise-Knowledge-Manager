NOVATECH SOLUTIONS
IT Security Policy

Employees must use strong passwords for company accounts.
Multi-factor authentication must be enabled wherever available.
Company credentials must never be shared with other employees.
Employees must not install unauthorized software on company devices.
Suspicious emails, links, or attachments must be reported to the IT team.
Company data must not be transferred to personal storage without authorization.
Security incidents should be reported to the IT team immediately.
