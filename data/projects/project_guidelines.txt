NOVATECH SOLUTIONS
Project Guidelines

All projects should have a documented objective, scope, owner, and timeline.
Project requirements must be documented before major development begins.
Major project decisions should be recorded in meeting notes.
Project owners are responsible for tracking milestones.
Changes to project scope should be communicated to relevant stakeholders.
Risks and blockers should be documented and reviewed regularly.
Completed projects should include a final summary and lessons learned.
Project documentation should be stored in the approved company knowledge repository.
