NOVATECH SOLUTIONS
Employee Onboarding SOP

Step 1: HR creates the employee record after joining confirmation.

Step 2: IT creates the employee's company email account and required system access.

Step 3: HR provides the employee handbook and company policies.

Step 4: The reporting manager introduces the employee to the team.

Step 5: The manager explains the employee's responsibilities and initial goals.

Step 6: IT provides required security and system training.

Step 7: The employee completes mandatory onboarding documentation.

Step 8: The manager conducts an onboarding review after the first two weeks.

All onboarding tasks should be tracked by HR until completion.
